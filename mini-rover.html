Mini Rover Challenge

RoboJackets, RoboNav Electrical Training final project, Fall 2026

The challenge

RoboJackets is running a Mini Rover Challenge. Four teams each start from pre-made rover hardware, choose the electrical components together, write Arduino code, and drive the rover over Bluetooth. Teams score points by completing tasks on a game field in timed attempts.

My role

We chose the components as a team. I drew our rover's electrical diagram and did the soldered wiring.

What I've done so far

The diagram starts from an 11 V battery and a fuse box. From the fuse box, power splits three ways: one buck converter for the two servos, one buck converter for the Arduino Uno, and a direct feed to two L298N motor drivers. The Arduino sends control signals to the servos and to the motor drivers, which run four DC motors. I also soldered the wiring.

What's next

Next I'm moving to the software side: programming the Arduino in the Arduino IDE so the rover can be driven over Bluetooth. I'll add the finished diagram, photos of the rover, and results to this page as the project moves forward.
